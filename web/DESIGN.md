# SOMA research dashboard

Direction: a professional planetary research workspace. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. The working surfaces use warm white and slate around a dark, neutral Moon scene. A persisted dark appearance supports long sessions; it changes the interface, never the scientific lighting.

The researcher's task is to select sourced locations, define an interval, run Python geometry, inspect the exact sample and preserve a reproducible result. The site catalog, Moon scene, numerical inspector and shared UTC dock each own one part of that task. No feature-card grid, invented KPI, decorative activity indicator or marketing hero belongs here.

- Warm working panels separate editable inputs and readable numerical evidence from the dark spatial scene.
- Slate typography and restrained amber selection make the current study and primary action easy to identify without making every label compete.
- The existing system reading face keeps controls familiar; tabular numerals align UTC epochs, angles, ranges and source hashes.
- A stronger title, distinct section headings, compact catalog rows and more generous field spacing establish hierarchy at research-tool density.
- The center scene gets the largest continuous area. Camera controls stay on the scene; scientific status and result quality stay outside it.
- The UTC dock groups playback, exact time, plot, comparison and reproducibility information. It remains usable when tiles fail.
- Light and dark appearances have complete text, control, chart and focus palettes. The scene and its neutral lighting remain visually stable across both appearances.
- Motion is limited to requested playback, camera transitions and immediate control feedback. Reduced motion retains static controls and pauses remain explicit.
- Native controls, visible labels, keyboard focus, scrolling source disclosures and separate mobile/tablet layouts are functional requirements.

No new logo, photograph, icon library or visual asset is introduced. Published catalog positions, native spacecraft histories and existing Moon tiles supply the product's visual content. The delivery archive is removed from public navigation; routing removal is handled separately.
