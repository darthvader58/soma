This is an automated independent review, not a separate human usability study. The Moon tiles were unavailable without a scoped token, and the text result remained available.

The relay network example retained its synthetic relay profile and station references throughout. The task was 16 MiB, with the same hardware, initial battery, analysis horizon and fixed 10:05 UTC deadline. The agent disabled the lunar-relay route and submitted at 08:50; it was feasible under assumptions, with a proven optimum and final arrival at 08:58:45 UTC. Enabling the same configured relay yielded a feasible proven optimum, with final arrival at 10:00:15 UTC.

The result displayed the two relay allocations with source transmission, receiver occupancy and byte availability. The uplink occupied 09:05:50–09:07:00 UTC, and the downlink 10:00:00–10:00:15 UTC. The archived replay records relay storage from 09:05:50 to 10:00:15 UTC. The UI showed synthetic service provenance, assumed net rates, ideal pointing, disabled synthetic light time, and the solver and fidelity labels. It also explicitly reported spacecraft state Unknown because stored trajectory states were missing. These are synthetic inputs, not lunar predictions.
